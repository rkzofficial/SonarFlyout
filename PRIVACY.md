# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

Sonar Flyout does not collect, store, or send any personal data.

- **No network access beyond your own PC.** The app talks only to SteelSeries GG's local server at `127.0.0.1` to read and change Sonar's volumes and devices. It makes no internet connections.
- **No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising.**
- **No accounts.**
- **Local settings only.** The only thing the app saves is whether it starts with Windows, which is stored by Windows itself.

Sonar Flyout reads SteelSeries GG's configuration file on your PC solely to find GG's local address. Your use of SteelSeries GG is covered by SteelSeries' own privacy policy.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/rkzofficial/SonarFlyout/issues.
