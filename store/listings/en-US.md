# Store listing: English (United States) (en-US)

## Product name
Sonar Flyout

## Short description
A native Windows 11 tray mixer for SteelSeries Sonar. Change channel volumes and outputs in one click.

## Description
Sonar Flyout puts SteelSeries Sonar one click away. Click its icon in the taskbar to adjust Master, Game, Chat, Media, Aux and Mic, mute any channel, or move every channel between your headphones, speakers and other devices, without opening SteelSeries GG.

It looks and behaves like Windows' own volume panel: Fluent design, light and dark theme, your language, and keyboard and screen reader support. When you want more control, unlink outputs to route each channel to its own device, just like Sonar's link toggle.

Sonar Flyout talks only to SteelSeries GG running on your PC and collects no data.

Requires SteelSeries GG with Sonar enabled in Classic mode.

## Product features
- Adjust Master, Game, Chat, Media, Aux and Mic volumes from the taskbar
- Mute or unmute any channel with one click
- Move every channel to another output device instantly
- Route each channel to its own device
- Choose the microphone Sonar uses
- Tray icon shows Sonar's master volume
- Light and dark theme, 13 languages, optional start with Windows
